# Revision of 29–30 September 2026

Implements the literature review of 29 September 2026 (research priorities and
paper revision guide). Everything below is reproducible with
`scripts/run_revision.sh` stages `data`, `gpu`, `cpu` (or the individual
scripts) and `analysis`; the paper is `docs/full paper.tex`.

## Results that change earlier claims

- The NB model matches the pinball GRU (within 0.04 MAE up to 4 weeks) but does
  not beat it. On the common cells it does not beat persistence in all seven
  years at h=1 (separate NB 15.94, p = 0.25; shared NB 16.63). The figures in
  `docs/negative_binomial.md` and `docs/new_findings_report.md` were measured on
  different cells and, for the four-horizon model, with the split leak above.
- The shared trunk and v3/v4 features make the NB model worse at short horizons.
- Observed weather beats the training climatology in all three families.
- After Holm, only Chronos-2 joint at h=3 is individually significant.
- On the consistent at-risk definition, onset ROC-AUC is 0.74–0.77 (PR-AUC
  0.41–0.47 vs 0.21 base rate); the earlier "near chance" (0.53–0.63) came from
  scoring onset weeks against all quiet weeks.
- 2026 hold-out: all five declared models beat persistence at every horizon;
  most 19-week bootstrap intervals include zero.

## Known test failure

`tests/test_model_tensors.py::test_known_gaps_are_carried_as_missing` asserts
the original GEE extraction ended 2026-04-26; the Open-Meteo file covers those
weeks. No result depends on them (they lie after every headline year, and the
hold-out weeks it covers are scored with weather present).
